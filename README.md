# Spudlatro - A Balatro Multiplayer Add-On.

### Developed By Dualsight
---
## Only tested and recommended for multiplayer.

Have you ever wanted more player interaction within Balatro Multiplayer? This mod is here to fill that niche!

This mod adds a new seal, the Potato Seal, that sends cards to your nemesis when played or enhanced. Cards sent are immediately removed from your deck and added when entering and leaving PvP Blind.

There are new jokers that revolve around Potatoes (and thus the new Potato Seal) and a new deck for ease of access of the new seal.
---
Requires Multiplayer 0.5.2 or above
---
### Jokers
Common: Wraith (Decrease the amount of money made by Potato Seals for your Nemesis when sold), Mr. Potato Head (Scored 7s gain a Potato Seal and are Retriggered)

Uncommon: Grandma's Mashed Potato Recipe (Retrigger right most joker if last played hand contained a Potato Seal)

Rare: Couch Potato (Potato Seals sent by your Nemesis have the Negative Edition, -1 Hand Size if your deck contains a Potato Seal)
